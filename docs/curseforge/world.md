# Folkhold World

## Summary

Factions, fortified villages, a simulated economy, caravans, raids and quests. The world changes without the player.

## Description

**A single-player world that changes without the player.** NPC factions trade, defend their villages and hold territory. Caravans travel between the villages. The reputation of the player decides how each village receives them.

### Features

- **A world that lives on its own.** A simulation runs the factions, the stocks and the caravans. It also runs in the chunks that the game does not load. The world around you changes with no visit.
- **Factions and territories.** Each village claims the territory around it. The claim grows with the level of the village, from a hamlet to a capital.
- **Fortified villages.** A village has a palisade with towers and gates, streets, homes and a village heart. A town, a city and a capital have outer rings of wall. Each culture has its own style of buildings.
- **A garrison.** Guards patrol, archers hold the towers and a captain leads. Healers treat the wounded units, riders patrol outside the walls, and golems guard the gates of the large villages. Kill the captain and destroy the heart: the village becomes ruins.
- **Village life.** Each household has a job and lives in the house-workshop of its job. The villagers work, go to the market at set hours and sleep at home. Builders repair the village and build its new buildings.
- **A simulated economy.** Each village produces and consumes goods with the jobs of its inhabitants. Its prices follow its stock and its needs.
- **Caravans and traveling merchants.** Trade between two villages sends a caravan on the road, by land or by boat.
- **Raids.** The raiders march on the gates of a faction village, and its garrison defends it.
- **Reputation and quests.** Each faction and each village remembers your actions. The quest board of a village gives deliveries and escorts from the state of the world.
- **Maps.** With Cartofold, the map shows the territories, the villages and the routes.
- **Two languages.** All the text of the mod exists in English and in French.

### Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.259 or later, Java 21.
- Required mods: Folkhold Core, Folkhold Blocks, GeckoLib and Cartofold.
- Client and server.
- Use a new world: the mod adds its villages to the world generation.

### Commands

The operators have commands and an admin screen (`/folkhold admin`). They show the simulation and teleport the operator to a village. `/folkhold village generate` places a village of a tier.
