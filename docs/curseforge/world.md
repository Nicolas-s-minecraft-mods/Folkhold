# Folkhold World

## Summary

Factions, fortified villages, a simulated economy, caravans, pillager camps, raids and quests. The world changes without the player.

## Description

**A single-player world that changes without the player.** NPC factions trade, defend their villages and hold territory. Caravans travel between the villages. The reputation of the player decides how each village receives them.

### Features

- **A world that lives on its own.** A simulation runs the factions, the stocks and the caravans. It also runs in the chunks that the game does not load. The world around you changes with no visit.
- **Factions and territories.** Each village claims the territory around it. The claim grows with the level of the village, from a hamlet to a capital.
- **Three cultures.** Valeholm (Norse long houses, axes), Ashmark (swords and crossbows) and Sunreach (flat houses, spears). Each culture has regions of the world, its buildings, its weapons and its trades. The villages use the wood and the stone of their biome.
- **Fortified villages.** A village has a palisade with towers and gates, streets, homes and a village heart. A town, a city and a capital have outer rings of wall. Villages stand on slopes, on cliffs, at the water and in the rock of the mountains. A maritime village has piers, a sea wall and houses on stilts.
- **A garrison.** Guards patrol, archers hold the towers and a captain leads. Healers treat the wounded units, riders patrol outside the walls, and golems guard the gates of the large villages. Kill the captain and destroy the heart: the village becomes ruins.
- **Village life.** Each household has a job and lives in the house-workshop of its job. Each bed of a home gives a place to an inhabitant. The workers work by day, trade at the market near noon and sleep at home at night.
- **Village growth.** The builders repair the village and build new homes, a market, fields and a windmill. A village changes its first palisade into a stone wall, and a town adds new rings and new storeys to its homes.
- **A simulated economy.** Each village produces and consumes goods with the jobs of its inhabitants. Its prices follow its stock and its needs. The villages have no food need: food is a trade good.
- **Caravans and traveling merchants.** Trade between two villages sends a caravan on the road, by land or by boat. With the Small Ships mod, a caravan crosses the water in one ship.
- **Pillager camps.** The land around each pillager outpost is pillager territory. Patrols walk around each camp. Stay in the territory and a patrol comes for you. A raid on a village near a camp comes from the side of the camp.
- **Raids.** The raiders force the gates of a faction village, and its garrison defends it. A village that falls loses experience and a part of its stocks.
- **Reputation and quests.** Each faction and each village remembers your actions. The quest board of a village gives deliveries, job quests of the workers, hunts of monsters and escorts of caravans. A quest gives emeralds, reputation, experience and sometimes items. A quest that you accept and do not complete removes reputation.
- **Journal.** The journal has tabs for the villages, the routes, the map and the quests. The Quests tab shows your active quests and the offers of the villages that you visited.
- **Maps.** With Cartofold, the maps show the explored territories, the villages and the route of the journal.
- **Data packs.** Data files give the quests, the village sizes, the jobs and the recipes of the economy.
- **Two languages.** All the text of the mod exists in English and in French.

### Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.259 or later, Java 21.
- Required mods: Folkhold Core, Folkhold Blocks, GeckoLib and Cartofold. The client also needs Confee: it shows the options screen.
- Optional mod: Small Ships (ships for the caravans).
- Client and server.
- Use a new world: the mod adds its villages to the world generation.

### Commands

The operators have commands and an admin screen (`/folkhold admin`). They show the simulation and teleport the operator to a village. `/folkhold village generate` places a village of a tier. The Config button opens the client options and the server options.
